# idle-dim

## What changed
- `config/dwm/bin/dwm-idle-dim` (new): xss-lock's notifier. It fades every on
  output to DIM_LEVEL (0.40) in 8 steps, then waits. On TERM/INT/HUP (xss-lock:
  input, or slock starting) it restores; it also exits once xss-lock itself
  is gone. Two paths:
  - night light's daemon running: it writes `<state>/nightlight/dim`
    ("<factor> <pid>") and calls `dwm-nightlight apply`. The daemon stays the
    one gamma writer, and its 60 s tick keeps the screen dim.
  - otherwise: it reads each output's Gamma and Brightness from
    `xrandr --verbose`, then sets `--gamma 1/g --brightness b*f` together.
- `config/dwm/bin/dwm-nightlight`: `dim_factor` folds the dim file into
  `brightness()` (floor 0.05), only while its pid lives, and deletes a file
  whose pid is dead.
- `config/dwm/bin/dwm-brightness`: `await_undim` before up/down/set. The
  dimmer's pid marker (`$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/dots/idle-dim.pid`) lasts until its
  restore is done, so the key that ends the dim reads the restored level.
- `config/dwm/bin/dwm-lock`: DIM_SECS=15, `xset s 585 15`,
  `xset s noblank` + `noexpose`, `xss-lock -n <dir>/dwm-idle-dim -- slock`.
  DIM_SECS=0 restores the old `xset s 600 600` with no notifier.
- Tests: `tests/dwm-idle-dim.sh` (new, 30 checks), `tests/dwm-lock.sh` (the new
  timer contract: lock = activation + cycle = 600 s).
- Docs: KEYBINDINGS § Lock and idle (table row, `noblank` paragraph, existing
  sessions), CHANGELOG, CLAUDE.md bin list.

## Why
Scope K slot 5, assumption A2. The probes are in the task's context.md:
- xserver source (dix/window.c, hw/xfree86/modes/xf86Crtc.c:3069): with the
  default prefer-blanking, screensaver activation calls xf86SaveScreen, i.e.
  DPMS Off on every output. A fade at activation would be invisible, and
  today's monitor already went off at 600 s, not at DPMS_OFF_SECS 660 as
  KEYBINDINGS claimed. noblank + noexpose draws nothing (SCREEN_ISNT_SAVED)
  while Xext/saver.c still sends ON and CYCLE to xss-lock.
- xrandr on Xvfb: Gamma reads back inverted, and `--brightness` alone resets
  gamma to 1.0, which would wipe night light's tint.

## Assumptions
- Type B: 15 s at 40%, fading in 0.8 s. Not asked: these are the constants
  at the top of the two scripts.
- Type B: an xss-lock already running at upgrade keeps no notifier until the
  next login. The re-armed timers make it lock at 585 s, undimmed, until
  then (KEYBINDINGS says so).
